# Measured and expected tire wear

A tire with usable tread measurements shows the observed wear rate and the expected rate side by side, with the total lifespan implied by each. The expected rate follows the tire's entered lifespan when available; otherwise it uses the average tread-wear rate. Without a usable measurement, this comparison is hidden.

The observed rate uses tread loss divided by distance driven while the tire was mounted. It is the raw measured rate, not the blended rate used by replacement forecasts. A measurement's weight in that forecast is shown as context; it is not a statistical confidence interval. The calculated total lifespans assume those rates continue and are not replacement promises. Recorded tread depth and the forecast calculation are unchanged. Telemetry-adjusted lifespan stays separate.

The delta compares wear rates, not remaining distance: positive means faster wear. Differences strictly above 25% are amber, strictly below -25% are green, and the band in between is neutral. These colors describe a comparison and do not change the tire's GOOD/WARNING/CRITICAL condition. Distances and wear-rate denominators follow the account's distance unit.

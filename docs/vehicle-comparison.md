# Vehicle comparison

Choose an electric, plug-in hybrid (PHEV) or range-extender (REEV) vehicle, open **Comparison**, and create a **tracked vehicle** comparison. All makes and models use the same calculation.

Set the conventional reference vehicle's fuel consumption in L/100 km, price per litre, purchase and resale prices, annual maintenance, insurance and taxes. Fuel-type presets are indicative starting points for France, not a model catalogue; replace them with assumptions appropriate to your vehicle, market and currency.

The tracked side uses recorded energy, maintenance and depreciation costs divided by known mileage, then applies the annual distance and duration you enter. Insurance is a fixed annual cost derived from current premiums and their recurrence or coverage period, independent of mileage. Hybrids include fill-ups and charging sessions. A fuel-price change applies to the fuel component of both vehicles; an electricity-price change applies only to charging. The fuel sensitivity chart changes both fuel components as well.

Results show energy costs, maintenance, insurance, depreciation, total cost, cost per distance and projected purchase break-even. Compare the two **Energy** rows for energy savings; total savings include the other cost categories. Recorded costs are left unchanged.

## Data and assumptions

- Record every fill-up and charging session, including free charges, and enter odometer readings. Missing costs or mileage make the estimate unreliable. A hybrid with only fuel or only charging records can be compared, but the calculation cannot infer unrecorded purchases.
- Costs represent purchases in the ledger, not energy measured while driving. Changes in fuel remaining in the tank or battery charge can bias a short history.
- Both sides use the same annual distance. With less than three months of history, annual distance defaults to 12,000 km; edit it to suit your use.
- Financing, loans and leases are excluded. Depreciation follows the existing purchase/resale calculation; break-even compares purchase plus running outlays without deducting resale.
- **Projection** remains a manually entered electric-versus-combustion scenario. A tracked hybrid comparison needs recorded hybrid data.

## Insurance projection

A current recurring premium is annualized by its billing interval: 100 per month, 300 per quarter and 1,200 every twelve months each project to 1,200 per year. A one-off premium with an explicit coverage period is annualized by that period while coverage is current. Amounts use the vehicle currency and the recorded exchange rate. Future and expired policies are excluded; set an end date on an obsolete recurring policy to avoid counting it alongside its replacement.

Payments without a recurrence or coverage period use their trailing twelve-month total as an annual estimate, with an assumption shown in the result. This estimate does not inflate a payment merely because the vehicle has a short tracking history. Multiple current premiums are added. Recorded TCO cash costs remain unchanged. Both comparison sides apply the same insurance inflation assumption; changing annual mileage never changes either insurance total.
